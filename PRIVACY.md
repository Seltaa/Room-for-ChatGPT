# Privacy Policy for Room for ChatGPT

Effective date: August 16, 2026

Room for ChatGPT is a browser customization extension. It changes the visual appearance of `chatgpt.com` and lets users configure themes, backgrounds, profile labels, avatars, a clock, and the message composer.

## Data collection

Room for ChatGPT does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or remotely process personal information, browsing history, authentication information, or chat content. The extension has no analytics, advertising, tracking, remote server, or third-party data service.

## Local data

The extension stores user-selected settings in `chrome.storage.local` on the user's current Chrome profile. This may include:

- Theme and appearance preferences
- User-provided display names and emoji
- User-selected avatar images
- User-selected background images or GIFs

This information remains on the user's device and is used only to provide the extension's customization features. It is not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

## Website access

Room for ChatGPT runs only on `https://chatgpt.com/*`. This access is required to apply the visual customizations selected by the user. The extension does not store or transmit the contents of ChatGPT conversations.

## Data deletion

Users can remove locally stored settings with the extension's **Reset everything** control. Uninstalling the extension also removes its locally stored extension data according to Chrome's extension storage behavior.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository with a revised effective date.
